2025

Heritage

Nesamovyto — meaning "uncontrollably" or "with abandon" in Ukrainian — needed a refresh that honored their cultural roots while speaking to contemporary audiences.

The existing brand felt static. The scarves themselves — wearable art inspired by Ukrainian cultural figures — demanded packaging and presentation that matched their ambition.

TLDR:

Refresh the brand identity so that it matches the uniqueness and expressiveness of the scarves it frames
